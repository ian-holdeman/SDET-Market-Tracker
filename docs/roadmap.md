# Product roadmap

The roadmap brings together current capabilities and future product plans. Technical evidence and its limitations are described in the linked public guides.

## Current capabilities

| Area | Features |
| --- | --- |
| Home | Market movers and the latest verified test-run summary, with dated saved evidence during refresh |
| The Board | Asset search, price charts, six price/activity metrics, finance links and private watchlists |
| The Tests | Published test results, retry history, nightly pipeline replay and four recorded demonstrations |
| The Logic | Project motivation, architecture, testing approach and AI-assistance disclosure |
| Settings | Browser appearance preferences, private watchlist clearing, account deletion and privacy information |
| Contact | Engineering inquiries, resume preview and explicit PDF download |

Public browsing does not require an account. Google sign-in enables private watchlists; the appearance toggle remains available to all visitors.

## Continuing improvements

- Broader market-data comparison and corporate-action coverage beyond the current limited provider sample.
- Additional individual test-case detail in pipeline reporting, while preserving honest incomplete and unavailable states.
- Ongoing accessibility, responsive-layout and device-compatibility coverage.
- Continued attention to low-cost hosting, provider availability and calendar coverage.

Future plans do not establish availability or a release date. Local tests and dated hosted observations each have specific limits; neither is a permanent production-health guarantee.
