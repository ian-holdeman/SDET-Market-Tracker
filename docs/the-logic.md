# The Logic

The Logic explains why the project was built, how its main systems fit together, how behavior is tested, and the role of AI assistance.

## Topics

- **Motivation:** a personal interest in markets combined with practical software-quality work.
- **Architecture:** market-data validation, authenticated private watchlists, public interfaces and trusted test evidence.
- **Testing:** deterministic checks, browser interactions, database integration and the limits of each evidence source.
- **AI and future work:** disclosed AI assistance and the planned direction of the application.

The page includes an accessible data-flow illustration and expandable evidence references. It does not provide visitor-triggered execution or simulated live results.

## Provenance and limits

Development used AI assistance, with feature design, code review and verification directed by the project owner. Visual inspection is distinct from an automated screenshot-comparison suite; a personal build timeline is not a measured productivity benchmark.

Market validation and the limited provider comparison do not establish universal financial accuracy. Recorded demonstrations use disclosed fixtures and are separate from published CI outcomes. Dated verification does not establish current hosted health.
